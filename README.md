# dakhabrakha discovery gacha

DakhaBrakha has such a unique and unpredictable sound. They define themselves as an ethno-chaos band, and I think the label really does it justice. But it's not just chaos, there's tremendous beauty in the kind of world music/fusion they do.

After being in their top 0.001% of their listeners on Spotify in 2021, I decided to create this "gacha" so other people could get to know them somewhat like I did. Since they're so genre-bending, upon coming into contact with them, I tried to make sense of the style of their songs, even to know when to listen to each of them, since I was so in awe. So I created several playlists by vibes: gut-punching/rollercoaster, love, atmospheric, dance, "personal powers" (in which each of the member's unique gifts shine), etc... So with this discovery game/gacha, not only can you dive into their albums and go blind, not knowing what to expect, but you can also choose a song I've curated into a category, which might be interesting to you.

I hope you can be open to, feel and enjoy the beauty and joy they radiate, especially during these times of rage, pain and war.
